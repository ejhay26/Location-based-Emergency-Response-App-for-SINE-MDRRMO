Emergency Triage & Disaster Protocol

Priority 1 • Immediate
RED: Critical / Life-Threatening

Unconscious individuals, severe hemorrhage, cardiac arrest, structural collapse, active structure fire with trapped citizens. Dispatch highest-tier team immediately with audible siren.

Priority 2 • Urgent
YELLOW: Serious / Delayed Risk

Stable bone fractures, moderate lacerations, rising flood water approaching homes without immediate submersion, vehicle collision with ambulatory victims. Dispatch within 10–15 mins.

Priority 3 • Minor / Non-Urgent
GREEN: Minor Injury / Hazard Clearing

Minor cuts, sprains, fallen tree branches blocking secondary alleys, non-critical localized debris. Route to Barangay DRRM volunteers or scheduled municipal clearing teams.
