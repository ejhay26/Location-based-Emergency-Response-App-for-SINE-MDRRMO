Quick Tips for ID Verification

To keep our community safe and make sure help goes to real people, MDRRMO requires a valid ID.

Bright Lighting: Take the photo in a bright place. Avoid shadows and strong camera flash glare over your info.
Clear & Readable: Make sure all 4 corners of your ID are inside the box and your name and ID number can be read clearly.
Selfie with ID: Hold your ID next to your chest or face. Look straight at the camera and make sure your fingers don't cover your name or photo.
